What’s stored, and where

Up to 2,000 hidden and archived posts within 6 MB stay on this device. Hidden posts expire after seven days; archived ones don’t. Your calls are kept separately (up to 2,000 within 2 MB), so archiving never erases what you taught the filter.

Improving rules uses your selected model: Claude sends examples and explanations to Anthropic; on-device processing keeps them local. X embeds load from X; saved media loads from its original host.
